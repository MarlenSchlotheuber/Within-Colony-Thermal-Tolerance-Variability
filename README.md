### Here the data as well as R-script for data analysis and creation of the figures of the manuscript "Exploring Within-Colony Thermal Tolernace Variability in Reef Building Corals" can be found.

1. The CBASS ED50 folder contains the raw photosynthetic efficiency data of the Coral Bleaching Automated Stress System (CBASS) experiments conducted in Indonesia and Malayisa. Here two excel tables for each sampling country can be found.

##### Note:
- In the manuscript colony 5 from Indonesia was removed due to sequencing errors. Therefore, colony 6 and 7 correspond to colony 5 and 6 in the manuscript.
- For Colony 2 from Malaysia, only four sampling regions (top, upper_middle1, lower_middle2, bottom) were included in the manuscript, instead of the six originally sampled, to have a consisten sampling strategy across colonies.

3. The CBASS HOBO folder contains the raw temperature data of the four CBASS tanks of each CBASS experiments conducted in Indonesia and Malaysia. Here two folders can be found: one containing the HOBO data of the one CBASS experiment from Indonesia and the HOBO data of the 6 CBASS experiments from Malaysia.
