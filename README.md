# Exploring Within-Colony Thermal Tolerance Variability in Reef-Building Corals
## Marlen Schlotheuber, Sebastian Szereday, Lisa Schaefer, Hannah Manns, Christian R. Voolstra

#### Here the R-script of the manuscript "Exploring Within-Colony Thermal Tolerance Variability in Reef Building Corals" can be found.

1. the R-script contains the script that was used to analyse the data and to create figures for the manuscript. Figures were finalized using Affinity Designer.

##### Note
- The ITS2 data of Indonesia and Malayisa was submitted to SymPortal in two individual runs. 
- Due to sequening errors of the 5th colony from Indonesia (top and bottom) no data is presented in the manuscript.
- Colony two from Malaysia was larger than the other colonies, therefore six sampling locations were sampled on the east and west side of the colony. In the manuscript only the first four sampling loactions were included, to ensure consistent sampling strategy (4 sampling locations, 1m distance between sampling locations) across the colony surface of each colony.
